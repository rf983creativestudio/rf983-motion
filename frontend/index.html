PROMPT DIRECTOR

Write for the camera.

A strong cinematic prompt describes more than a subject. Build it in layers:

  1. Subject and action — who or what is visible, and exactly what changes.
  2. Environment — location, weather, depth and background details.
  3. Framing — wide, medium, close-up, angle and composition.
  4. Camera movement — locked-off, dolly, tracking, crane or orbit.
  5. Light and texture — source, direction, contrast, atmosphere and material.
  6. Temporal logic — one clear action that unfolds in a readable order.

Prompt assistance here is a local writing guide, not an AI model call.
